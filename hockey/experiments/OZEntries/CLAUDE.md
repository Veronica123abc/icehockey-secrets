In playsequence_compiled.json, the events representing an OZ entry defined as follows:

Carry into OZ:
   name="controlledentry"
   type="carry"

Pass into OZ:
   name="controlledentry"
   type="pass"

Dumpin into OZ:
   name="dumpinentry"
   
   
To compute DZ exits to match each entry, use the following definitions:
   name="dumpout" or name="controlledexit"
