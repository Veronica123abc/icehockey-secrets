"Against" Events
Controlled Entries Against: At the moment of a controlled entry AGAINST, the closest defender is credited (with their location) for an entry AGAINST
Dump-Ins Against: At the moment of an opposition dump-in, the closest defender in the lane is credited (with their location) for a dump-in AGAINST.
Entry Denials: A DZ entry denial is credited to the defender tagged with the entry AGAINST if the opposition loses possession before entering the zone, or if they lose possession on the first play after an entry.
Shot Pressure: An opposition shot is deemed pressured when, at the time of shot release, a defender is in front of and within 8 feet of the shooter, or behind the shooter and within 3 feet. Absolute distance is also tracked.

Blocks
Blocked Pass: When an opposition pass attempt hits a player's stick or body, preventing the pass from being received by its target. A failed blocked pass is tagged when a completed pass is touched by a defender on the way to the receiver, or when the puck travels between a player's legs or under their stick.
Blocked Shot: When an opposition shot attempt hits a player's stick or body, preventing the shot from reaching the net. A failed blocked shot is tagged when a shot on net is touched by a defender on the way to the receiver, or when the puck travels between a player's legs or under their stick.
Blueline Hold: When a player (usually a defenseman) blocks a dump-out (or certain pass attempts to the NZ), keeping the puck in the OZ.
Reception Prevention: When an opposition pass attempt is prevented from being received by its target by a player making contact with the intended receiver (stick lifts, stick jams, body contact, etc).

Checks
Body Check: When a player uses their body to separate the puck from an opposition player. Note: our system does not count hits after a player is no longer in possession of the puck. Only hits that remove possession are counted.
Stick Check: When a player uses their stick to separate the puck from an opposition player.
