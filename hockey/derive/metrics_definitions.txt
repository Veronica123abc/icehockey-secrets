

In table 'event' the relevant columns are: name, outcome, type, shorthand, playsection, flags
The location from where a shot is taken from one of: outside, slot, inner slot
The result of a shot is one of: 'blocked', 'missed', 'successful', 'failed'
The number of failed shots is always the number of blocked shots + the number of missed shots
A shot attempt (failed or successful) has 'name'='shot'
A shot from slot has 'name'='shot' and 'type'='slot'
A shot from inner slot has 'name'='shot', type='slot', 'playsection'='innerSlot'
A deflected shot attempt has 'deflected' in the list of strings in column 'flags'
A 1-timer shot attempt has '1timer' in the list of strings in column 'flags'
