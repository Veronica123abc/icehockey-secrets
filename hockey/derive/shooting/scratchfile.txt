In table 'event' the relevant columns are: name, outcome, type, shorthand, playsection, flags


There are four main categories of shots:
A Shot Attempt has 'name'='shot'
A Regular Goal has 'name'='shot' and 'withgoal' in flags
A 1 Timers Shot has 'name'='shot' and '1timer' in flags
A Shot With Pressure has 'name'='shot' and 'pressure' in flags
A Shot With No Pressure has 'name'='shot' but not 'pressure' in flags
A Deflected Shot has 'name'='shot' and 'deflected' in flags

A shot can be taken from one of three locations:
Outside Slot has type='outside'
Slot has type='slot'
Inner Slot has type='slot' and play_section='innerSlot'

A successful shot has 'outcome'='successful'
A failed shot has 'outcome'='failed'
Failed shots are either blocked or missed
A blocked shot from Outside Slot has 'type'='outsideblocked'
A blocked shot from Slot has 'type'='slotblocked'
A blocked shot from Inner Slot has 'type'='slotblocked' and 'play_section'='innerSlot'
A Missed Shot (of any type) has outcome='failed'
The number of failed shots is always the number of blocked shots + the number of missed shots
