Passes are filtered as follows:
Outcome
    successful
    failed
Zone
    DZ (defensive)
    NZ (neutral)
    OZ (offensive)
Type
    Outlet
    Stretch
    D2D (defense to defense)
    North
    South
    East-to-West
    Off the Rush
    Slot

In practice, that yeilds the following possible pass-types (each either successful or failed)
DZ D2D pass,
DZ outlet pass,
DZ stretch pass,
DZ stretch entry pass,
NZ south pass,
NZ north pass,
NZ east-west pass,
NZ entry pass,
OZ pass off the rush,
OZ south cycle pass,
OZ north cycle pass,
OZ east-west point pass,
OZ pass to the slot


Further - passes can also be filtered on:
    entrypass (if "entry" is in type)
    offboards (if "offboards" is in type)
    direct (not offboards)
    
Finally, RECEPTIONS (name="reception" are filtered as:

outcome 
    successful
    failed
zone
    DZ
    NZ
    OZ
    slot

type
    OZEntry
    Not OZ Entry
